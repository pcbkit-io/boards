# Delta-S02 (Дельта-С-02)

The ZX Spectrum 48K compatible home computer Elara built in Cheboksary in 1993, drawn by PCBKit from
its factory documents: 52 Soviet chips and no ULA, run chip by chip from the board's netlist with
the standard 48K ROM; The Scorched Earth on its cassette.

- Author: PCBKit
- License: CC0-1.0

This project began as the demo “Delta-S02” of PCBKit (https://pcbkit.io):
https://app.pcbkit.io/demo/delta-s02. The circuit and the parts' places are read from the factory's
«Схема электрическая принципиальная БПК Дельта-С» and «Плата процессора БПК Дельта-С-02», scanned and
shared on zx-pk.ru (https://zx-pk.ru/threads/34820); the connectors' pinouts from the Omsk archive of
Andrey Pikalo. Placed as the factory's drawing places it and routed by PCBKit.

Not part of this board's licence, fetched from where they are published: the 48K ROM (© Amstrad, its
distribution permitted for emulators) and The Scorched Earth (X-Project Association, 1997, freeware;
https://worldofspectrum.net/item/0013039/).
