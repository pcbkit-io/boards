# CO₂ monitor

Sensirion's SCD40 measures CO₂, temperature and humidity every five seconds; the OLED shows the concentration with a ten-minute graph and a traffic light says when to open a window. Raise the CO₂ in the lab, as a full room does, and watch it turn red.
