# Breakout

Breakout on the pocket console: move the paddle with left and right, serve with X; five rows of bricks, three balls, the angle off the paddle set by where the ball lands.

- Author: PCBKit
- License: CC0-1.0

This project began as the demo “Breakout” of PCBKit (https://pcbkit.io): https://app.pcbkit.io/demo/breakout. Placed and routed by PCBKit; the sketch builds and runs in the browser.
