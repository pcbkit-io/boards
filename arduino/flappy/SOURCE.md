# Flappy bird

A flappy bird on the pocket console: X flaps, gravity does the rest, pipes scroll in with a gap to fly through; one point a pipe and the best kept in the EEPROM.

- Author: PCBKit
- License: CC0-1.0

This project began as the demo “Flappy bird” of PCBKit (https://pcbkit.io): https://app.pcbkit.io/demo/flappy. Placed and routed by PCBKit; the sketch builds and runs in the browser.
