# LCD runner

The classic 16 × 2 character LCD on LiquidCrystal's wiring: a dinosaur made of custom characters runs along the bottom line, jumps cacti with the button and runs under birds; turn the trimmer and the contrast changes as on the real module.

- Author: PCBKit
- License: CC0-1.0

This project began as the demo “LCD runner” of PCBKit (https://pcbkit.io): https://app.pcbkit.io/demo/lcd-runner. Placed and routed by PCBKit; the sketch builds and runs in the browser.
