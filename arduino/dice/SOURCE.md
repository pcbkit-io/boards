# Dice

Dice on the pocket console: X rolls one to five dice that tumble before they settle, up and down choose how many, Z shows how often each sum came up.

This project began as the demo “Dice” of PCBKit (https://pcbkit.io): https://app.pcbkit.io/demo/dice. Placed and routed by PCBKit; the sketch builds and runs in the browser.
