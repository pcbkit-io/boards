# Simon

Simon on a round board: four coloured lights, each with its button; the Nano plays a sequence and you repeat it, one more every round, a little faster. Keys 1 to 4 press the buttons; the best round stays in the EEPROM.

This project began as the demo “Simon” of PCBKit (https://pcbkit.io): https://app.pcbkit.io/demo/simon. Placed and routed by PCBKit; the sketch builds and runs in the browser.
