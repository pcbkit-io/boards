# Falling blocks

Falling blocks on the pocket console: a 10 × 20 well beside the next piece and the score; left and right move, X turns, down drops faster, Z drops at once; full lines clear and the pace rises.

- Author: PCBKit
- License: CC0-1.0

This project began as the demo “Falling blocks” of PCBKit (https://pcbkit.io): https://app.pcbkit.io/demo/tetris. Placed and routed by PCBKit; the sketch builds and runs in the browser.
