# Magic drawing screen

Two knobs draw on an OLED, one across and one up and down, as on the toy; the button wipes the picture. Turn the knobs in the lab.

This project began as the demo “Magic drawing screen” of PCBKit (https://pcbkit.io): https://app.pcbkit.io/demo/etch-a-sketch. Placed and routed by PCBKit; the sketch builds and runs in the browser.
