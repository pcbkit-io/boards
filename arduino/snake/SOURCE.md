# Snake

Snake on the pocket console, an Arduino Nano behind a 0.96" OLED with a D-pad and two buttons: steer with the arrow keys, eat, grow and go faster; the best score stays in the EEPROM.

- Author: PCBKit
- License: CC0-1.0

This project began as the demo “Snake” of PCBKit (https://pcbkit.io): https://app.pcbkit.io/demo/snake. Placed and routed by PCBKit; the sketch builds and runs in the browser.
