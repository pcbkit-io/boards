# USB-C serial adapter

A USB-C to UART adapter composed from the standard circuit blocks: the Type-C port with its CC resistors and ESD array, a 3.3 V regulator, the USB to UART bridge, a power LED and the header. The blocks sized and typed every part; the engine placed them.

This project began as the demo “USB-C serial adapter” of PCBKit (https://pcbkit.io): https://app.pcbkit.io/demo/usb-uart. Placed and routed by PCBKit.
