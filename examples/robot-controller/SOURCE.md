The design was converted from the KiCad board by `pcbkit import kicad --no-routes`:
components, footprints, placement, outline, nets, rules and pours are kept; tracks, vias and pour fills
are stripped. The original author is credited above; this derived work is shared under the same license.
Original board: 4 copper layers, 187 connections, stratum 4/medium.

## The demo

This project began as the demo “Robot controller” of PCBKit (https://pcbkit.io): https://app.pcbkit.io/demo/robot-controller. The parts and outline are the original's; the tracks and vias were routed by PCBKit.
