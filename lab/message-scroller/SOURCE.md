# Message scroller

Type into the serial console and your words scroll across eight seven-segment digits, letters included, in a font of the program's own. A UART receive interrupt collects the line; the knob sets the speed.

This project began as the demo “Message scroller” of PCBKit (https://pcbkit.io): https://app.pcbkit.io/demo/message-scroller. Placed and routed by PCBKit; the firmware runs in the browser.
