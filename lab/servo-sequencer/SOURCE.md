# Servo sequencer

Three servos from one timer interrupt. They dance a built-in choreography until you press TEACH: move them with the three knobs, press again and they replay your motion in a smooth loop.

- Author: PCBKit
- License: CC0-1.0

This project began as the demo “Servo sequencer” of PCBKit (https://pcbkit.io): https://app.pcbkit.io/demo/servo-sequencer. Placed and routed by PCBKit; the firmware runs in the browser.
