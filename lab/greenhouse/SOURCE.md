# Greenhouse controller

A house-shaped board that opens its window: an SHT31 reads the temperature, an SCD40 the CO₂, and a servo opens the window when it is too warm or stuffy. Turn the setpoint knob, change the temperature and CO₂ in the lab, and watch it react.

This project began as the demo “Greenhouse controller” of PCBKit (https://pcbkit.io): https://app.pcbkit.io/demo/greenhouse. Placed and routed by PCBKit; the firmware runs in the browser.
