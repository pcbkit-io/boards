# Reaction game

How fast are you? Press to start: red, amber, a random wait, then green and GO. Press again and the display shows your time in milliseconds and your best; pressing before green is a false start.

This project began as the demo “Reaction game” of PCBKit (https://pcbkit.io): https://app.pcbkit.io/demo/reaction-game. Placed and routed by PCBKit; the firmware runs in the browser.
