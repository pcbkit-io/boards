# LED ring

Twelve lights round a black disc, each dimmed by software PWM from a timer interrupt: a spinning comet with a fading tail, a dial gauge of the knob from green to red, a breathing wave. The button changes the mode, the knob the speed.

This project began as the demo “LED ring” of PCBKit (https://pcbkit.io): https://app.pcbkit.io/demo/led-ring-lab. Placed and routed by PCBKit; the firmware runs in the browser.
