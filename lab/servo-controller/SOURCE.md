# Servo controller

A device you can run: an ATmega328P reads a knob and a button and drives a servo with PWM, sleeping between interrupts. Turn the knob or press the button and watch the servo follow.

This project began as the demo “Servo controller” of PCBKit (https://pcbkit.io): https://app.pcbkit.io/demo/servo-controller. Placed and routed by PCBKit; the firmware runs in the browser.
