# Serial command device

A device you talk to over UART: an ATmega168P echoes every byte and turns its light on for 1 and off for 0. Type into its serial console and watch it answer.

This project began as the demo “Serial command device” of PCBKit (https://pcbkit.io): https://app.pcbkit.io/demo/serial-device. Placed and routed by PCBKit; the firmware runs in the browser.
