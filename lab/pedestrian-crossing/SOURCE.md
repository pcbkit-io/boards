# Pedestrian crossing

A traffic light for cars and one for people, as a state machine: press PUSH and the countdown starts, cars get amber and red, WALK shows the seconds left and flashes before it ends.

- Author: PCBKit
- License: CC0-1.0

This project began as the demo “Pedestrian crossing” of PCBKit (https://pcbkit.io): https://app.pcbkit.io/demo/pedestrian-crossing. Placed and routed by PCBKit; the firmware runs in the browser.
