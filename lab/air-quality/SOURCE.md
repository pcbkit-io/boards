# Air quality meter

A battery-powered air meter you can run: an ATmega328P with real C firmware reads an SHT31 and an SCD40 and shows temperature and humidity on a MAX7219 display, with green, orange and red CO₂ lights. Change the weather and watch the firmware answer.

This project began as the demo “Air quality meter” of PCBKit (https://pcbkit.io): https://app.pcbkit.io/demo/air-quality. Placed and routed by PCBKit; the firmware runs in the browser.
